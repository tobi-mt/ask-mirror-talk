# Mirror Talk 8.34.0

Intentional Living Question placement and attention-design release.

- Moves the homepage question from the early discovery path to the natural bridge between topic exploration and archive recommendations.
- Places the inline question around 46% of actual reading progress, at a safe top-level section or paragraph boundary.
- Limits the acceptable insertion window to 30–68% so the question does not interrupt the opening or compete with the conclusion.
- Leaves short or structurally ambiguous documents uninterrupted instead of forcing a card at the end.
- Keeps one primary action inside articles: **Explore the answer**. Daily reminder enrollment remains on the homepage, where the visitor has broader intent.
- Uses a compact inline presentation with lower visual weight, shorter supporting copy and no additional SEO heading.
- Preserves the full homepage treatment as a deliberate transition from browsing needs to personal reflection.
- Retains the consent-safe notification recovery and subscription observability introduced in 8.33.
