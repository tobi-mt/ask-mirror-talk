# Mirror Talk Theme 7.5.0 — Living Reflection Workspace

The Living Mirror now keeps podcast listening and reflection in one continuous interface.

- **Reflect on this moment** pauses the episode and opens an in-player reflection workspace.
- The selected episode and exact timestamp are submitted automatically after the visitor explicitly chooses to reflect.
- The resulting reflection appears inside The Living Mirror without a page redirect.
- Visitors can choose suggested follow-ups or write their own follow-up questions.
- **Save reflection** stores the result privately in the browser's existing Insights collection.
- **Return to listening** restores the same episode and timestamp, resuming only when the episode was playing before reflection.
- Includes loading, failure, retry, privacy and non-clinical guidance states.
- Responsive layouts were reviewed at desktop and 390px mobile widths.

The homepage structure and the standalone Ask Mirror Talk experience remain unchanged.
