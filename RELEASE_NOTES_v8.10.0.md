# Mirror Talk 8.10.0

## Governed Automatic Editorial Composer

- Keeps every existing post and page URL immutable during automatic optimization.
- Preserves canonical `post_content`; visible enhancements are server-rendered for readers and crawlers without destructive body rewrites.
- Records the source-content hash, word count, protected URL, generated decisions, timestamp, and responsible administrator.
- Adds a searchable, paginated administrator work ledger covering processed posts and pages.
- Adds correction controls for search titles, descriptions, visible introductions, takeaways, and focus phrases.
- Records approvals, corrections, recompositions, disabled decisions, re-enabled decisions, and restorations.
- Retains per-decision revert and complete prior-state restoration.
- Uses complete extracted sentences for introductions and takeaways to prevent incomplete thoughts.
- Reprocesses existing Composer entries under the new governed rules while retaining history.

The Composer never rewrites quotations, transcripts, embeds, factual source statements, slugs, or permalink structures.
