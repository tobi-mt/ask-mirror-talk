# Mirror Talk Theme 8.2.4

## AI visibility that reports evidence, not promises

- Adds a privacy-preserving, first-party AI visibility dashboard for the most recent 30 days.
- Counts recognized AI crawler user agents separately from visits referred by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot and Gemini.
- Shows the public landing pages that receive identifiable AI referrals.
- Adds an automatically calculated AI citation-readiness score based on source ownership, discovery controls, structured data, connected search properties, descriptions and featured images.
- Stores only daily aggregate counts and landing paths for up to 120 days—never IP addresses, prompts, cookies or private Ask Mirror Talk reflections.
- Excludes general Googlebot and Bingbot traffic from AI-only crawler counts.
- Explains the unavoidable measurement boundary: crawler requests and referrals do not prove a generated answer contained a citation, while Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster do not expose complete AI-answer citation data.

## Existing citation foundations retained

- Owned `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` source guides.
- Canonical URLs, social preview images and podcast/article/entity structured data.
- Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster synchronization.
- XML, news and video sitemaps, IndexNow, RSS attribution and explicit AI crawler controls.

No administrator configuration is required for first-party observation. Verified external search connections continue synchronizing on their existing schedule.
