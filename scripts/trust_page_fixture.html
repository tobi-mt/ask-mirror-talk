Policy updated October 2026

Mirror Talk publishes original conversations and editorial material intended to support reflection, learning and personal growth.

Accuracy and attribution

Guest statements are attributed to their speakers. Quotations, credentials and sources are published only when they can be verified.

Independence and safety

Editorial decisions are made for listeners and readers, not to manipulate search rankings.

Corrections
