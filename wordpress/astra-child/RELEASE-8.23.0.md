# Mirror Talk 8.23.0

## Content-led editorial presentation system

- Replaces repetitive archive cards with a shared, semantic episode/article card.
- Selects compact, editorial or deep-dive presentation from RSS duration profiles
  and Composer source length.
- Reserves cinematic presentation for sticky, explicitly featured, or high-scoring
  long conversations with a real featured image.
- Creates varied but deterministic twelve-column archive compositions across the
  blog, categories, tags, dates, authors and virtual topic hubs.
- Adds useful topic, publication date, reading time, duration, transcript and
  listen/read context without changing URLs or navigation.
- Gives qualifying individual conversations a cinematic split hero while preserving
  their sole H1 and original article body.
- Collapses every presentation to an edge-to-edge single column on small screens.
- Disables ornamental movement for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

The system reuses the established Mirror Talk palette, typography, abstract imagery,
Story Studio and Composer evidence. It introduces no new tracking or personalization.
