# Mirror Talk 8.23.1

Live verification showed that the latest production articles have similar source
lengths, so their accurate `editorial` classification produced an unnecessarily
uniform two-column archive. This refinement separates content depth from visual role:

- The newest conversation becomes the lead composition without being mislabelled as
  a deep-dive or major feature.
- The second conversation becomes a supporting composition.
- Remaining cards continue to use compact, editorial and deep-dive content profiles.
- Verified major conversations still take precedence and receive cinematic treatment.
- Tablet and mobile layouts collapse these roles safely with no horizontal overflow.
