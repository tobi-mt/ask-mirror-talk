# Mirror Talk 8.30.0 — Page-One Growth

This release adds a governed organic-growth layer to Mirror Talk Intelligence.

- Ranks attainable high-intent Google and Bing queries, prioritizing positions 4–20.
- Detects search cannibalization without automatically deleting, redirecting, or changing URLs.
- Audits episode completeness across questions, transcripts, timestamps, quotations, sources, video, podcast audio, and internal links.
- Tracks separate readiness for Google, Bing, video search, podcast discovery, and AI citations.
- Detects meaningful 28-day search declines and queues evidence-led refreshes.
- Creates reversible supporting-conversation links from closely related published content.
- Generates guest/source outreach suggestions without sending messages or fabricating endorsements.
- Reuses the existing guarded title experiment thresholds and automatic rollback.
- Runs the existing broken-link crawler after each safe growth cycle.
- Adds an auditable Page-One Growth panel to Mirror Talk Intelligence.

Safety rules remain strict: visible headlines and public URLs are not changed by this engine; transcripts, quotations, credentials, claims, sources, and backlinks are never invented.
