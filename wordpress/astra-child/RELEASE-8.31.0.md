# Mirror Talk 8.31.0 — Contextual Video Moments

- Gives a post- or page-specific YouTube URL priority over the channel fallback.
- Detects source videos in WordPress content, owned video shortcodes and legacy Elementor data.
- Automatically enables Story Studio on pages that demonstrably own a video while leaving unrelated pages quiet.
- Places the player at a safe editorial boundary before the second substantive section, or before related/reflection content on shorter documents.
- Respects an existing embedded player so a document never receives a duplicate video module.
- Continues to use the latest Mirror Talk upload only when no document-specific video is available.
