# Mirror Talk 8.32.0 — Owned Listen & Connect Hub

- Adds a first-party, server-rendered `/links/` destination to replace Linktree and Linkfire.
- Uses the official podcast cover art in an optimized 1000 × 1000 social and hero asset.
- Plays the latest RSS episode and allows visitors to choose from recent episodes without leaving the page.
- Reuses verified listening destinations and intelligently orders them for the visitor's device and remembered preference.
- Connects directly to Ask Mirror Talk, the owned newsletter form, the episode archive, *Confessions*, contact, WhatsApp, Patreon, YouTube and approved social profiles.
- Adds native share/copy support and campaign-tagged external destinations.
- Records consent-approved link-hub actions inside Mirror Talk Intelligence and exposes the ranked actions in the dashboard and CSV export.
- Adds a canonical URL, search/social description, podcast cover social card, `CollectionPage`/`ItemList` structured data and sitemap coverage.
- Suppresses the duplicate floating podcast/Ask controls and heavy YouTube studio on this focused conversion surface.
- Includes responsive, keyboard, contrast and reduced-motion treatment without a page-builder dependency.
